## About Me

I'm a **Frappe Framework and ERPNext developer** focused on building and customizing ERP systems around real business processes.

My work involves turning business requirements into practical ERP solutions using custom applications, DocTypes, workflows, permissions, reports, print formats, integrations, and automation.

I enjoy working close to both the **business process and the code** — understanding how a company operates, then building the system around that process.

## Featured Projects

### 🔬 Histopathology Laboratory ERP

A customized **ERPNext/Frappe system for histopathology laboratory operations**.

The project focuses on building a complete operational workflow around laboratory visits, samples, diagnosis, billing, commissions, and reporting.

**Key areas:**

- Laboratory Visit management
- Accession and sample processing
- Laboratory workflow automation
- Diagnosis workflow
- Referral doctor management
- Commission and payment logic
- Role-based permissions
- Reports
- Custom print formats
- QR/barcode workflows
- ERPNext accounting integration

**Stack:** `Frappe` `ERPNext` `Python` `JavaScript` `MariaDB`

---

### 🚗 Automotive Spare Parts ERP

An **ERPNext implementation and customization project** for an automotive spare-parts business.

The system covers the main business operations from purchasing and inventory to sales and accounting.

**Key areas:**

- Item and inventory management
- Buying
- Selling
- Stock management
- Price lists
- Customer management
- Accounting configuration
- Business process configuration
- User permissions

**Stack:** `ERPNext` `Frappe` `Python` `Linux`

## Development Approach

> Understand the business process first. Then build the software around it.

I prefer building ERP systems that are **simple, maintainable, and aligned with how people actually work**.

Instead of adding customization for the sake of customization, I focus on understanding the underlying process and using Frappe/ERPNext to make that process more reliable and efficient.
